# Global Ads Analytics Dashboard – Case Study

## 1. Background

Companies running global ads often spend money across multiple platforms and regions, but they don’t always have a clear idea of what is actually working.

This project was built to analyze global ad performance and make sense of how different campaigns are performing across locations and channels.

---

## 2. Problem

Marketing budgets are usually distributed globally, but performance is uneven.

The main problems were:

* No clear view of high and low performing regions
* Difficulty in comparing campaigns across platforms
* Unclear relationship between spend and conversions
* Lack of simple reporting for decision makers

The goal was to fix this using a simple dashboard.

---

## 3. Data

The dataset included:

* Campaign names
* Ad platforms
* Region / country
* Impressions
* Clicks
* Conversions
* Spend
* Revenue

It was cleaned and structured before building the dashboard.

---

## 4. Approach

### Step 1: Data cleaning

Removed missing values, fixed inconsistent formats, and standardized columns.

### Step 2: KPI creation

Created basic metrics like:

* CTR (Click Through Rate)
* Conversion Rate
* ROI / ROAS
* Cost per click

### Step 3: Dashboard building

Built an interactive Power BI dashboard to explore:

* Region performance
* Campaign comparison
* Spend vs return patterns

---

## 5. Findings

### Regional performance is not equal

Some regions consistently perform better in terms of conversions, even with lower spend. This suggests audience quality matters more than budget size.

---

### High impressions don’t guarantee results

A few campaigns had strong impressions but weak conversions. This points to issues in targeting or messaging.

---

### A small number of campaigns drive most results

Performance is not evenly distributed. A few campaigns are responsible for most of the conversions and revenue.

---

### Spending is not always efficient

Higher spend campaigns did not always give better ROI. Some low-budget campaigns performed better overall.

---

## 6. Recommendations

* Shift more budget toward high-performing regions
* Reduce spending on low-ROI campaigns
* Improve targeting for high-impression low-conversion ads
* Continuously monitor campaign performance instead of one-time analysis
